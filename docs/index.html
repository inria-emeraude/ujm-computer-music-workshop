Computer Music Workshop

The Computer Music Workshop course is offered as part of the CCNT Master's program at Jean Monnet University in Saint-Étienne (France). Its purpose is to provide theoretical and practical foundations for computer programming in the context of real-time audio signal processing. Topics include sampling theory, the manipulation of digital signals for sound synthesis and processing, the Faust programming language, and real-time control of these systems. By the end of the course, students will be able to create their own programs for sound processing and synthesis.

The following topics are covered (with some flexibility ;) ):

  • Digital audio fundamentals (sampling)
  • Introduction to programming in Faust
  • Faust Web IDE
  • Using elements from the libraries
  • Basic signal operations (connection, split, merge, etc.)
  • Basic mathematical operations
  • etc.
  • Fundamental sound synthesis and processing techniques in Faust (signal processing)
  • Additive synthesis
  • Subtractive synthesis
  • AM
  • FM
  • Basic filters
  • Distortion
  • Flanger
  • Echo
  • Using Faust to create plug-ins, externals, etc.
  • Controlling objects produced with Faust (MIDI + microcontrollers)

Schedule and Course Material

The course takes place during the first 6 weeks of the semester on Wednesdays 9-11am starting on September 9th, 2026.

All the course material is available on this website in the lectures tab in the menu above.

The course is organized into 4 lectures spread out over 6 sessions. Take home assignments are assigned at the end of each lecture and are due 2 weeks later.

Assessment Criteria

  • Continuous assessment: 60%
  • Attendance: -20% for each unjustified absence
  • One take-home assignment (mini-project) at the end of each lecture. Students have 2 weeks to submit their assignment. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Final project: 40%
  • Resit exam: noooo pleeeaaaase :((
